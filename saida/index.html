Mirai

Mirai is a personal automation tool operated by a single independent creator. It connects to the creator's own YouTube channel to upload videos the creator produced, to read the channel's public and analytics statistics (such as audience retention), and to help the creator improve future videos. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.

Português

Mirai é uma ferramenta pessoal de automação operada por um único criador independente.
