Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mirai ("the app") handles information obtained through Google APIs.

What the app accesses

With the explicit permission of the account owner, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes on the owner's own YouTube channel:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos created by the owner.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel name, video list and public statistics.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the channel's analytics (for example audience retention and search terms) so the owner can improve content.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to provide these features to the account owner. Access and refresh tokens, and the analytics results, are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files protected by operating-system permissions. They are not uploaded to any server operated by us, not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. No human other than the account owner reads this data.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Some text produced by the app (for example video scripts) may be generated with third-party AI services; no Google user data other than aggregate channel statistics is included in those requests, and no OAuth token is ever sent to them.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the stored tokens by disconnecting the account in the app or deleting its local files. Questions: newvitor30@gmail.com.
